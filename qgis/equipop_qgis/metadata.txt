about=For QGIS 3.38 to 4.x. Developed and tested on QGIS 3.42; 3.38 is the declared minimum - that is where QgsField moved to QMetaType and where the deprecated parameterAsFields could be dropped. Written with the scoped Qgis enums that QGIS 4 (Qt 6) requires. EquiPop measures who and what is around every point using a neighbourhood of the k nearest people rather than an administrative boundary - so results do not depend on where somebody drew the lines. Tools 1 and 2 are shown; tools 3 to 6 (raster curation, demography, downloads, folder inventory) are hidden until you run '0. Show or hide tools 3-6'. Requires the equipop Python package in QGIS's own Python: python -m pip install equipop
